# Data sources — gmp_household (retrieved 2026-10-21; free public data, no keys)
Scripts: ../code/fetch_*.py (clean step); raw files in raw/<source>/ (downloaded with curl, URLs below). Clean outputs in clean/.

## 1. OECD analytical house price indicators
- URL: https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.ECO.MPD,DSD_AN_HOUSE_PRICES@DF_HOUSE_PRICES,1.0/all?format=csvfilewithlabels
- Raw: raw/oecd_hpi/an_house_prices.csv. Clean: clean/oecd_house_prices.csv (iso3,freq,period,variable,variable_label,unit,base_period,adjustment,value). Script fetch_oecd_hpi.py.
- Variables: HPI nominal house price index; RPI rent price index; HPI_RPI price-to-rent ratio (index); RHP real HPI; HPI_YDH price-to-income; *_AVG = % of long-term average. All IX (2015=100) except *_AVG.
- Coverage: 50 areas (includes aggregates such as euro area/OECD; filter by iso3). HPI: A 1960-2025, Q 1960Q1-2026Q3 (JPN from 1960; most from 1970; PRT from 1988). RPI: 1956-2025. Price-to-rent: 45 areas from 1968/69. All 18 priority countries present.
- Terms: OECD Terms and Conditions (free reuse with attribution); https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html
- Quirks: series are seasonally adjusted; annual = average of quarters; national definitions differ (existing vs new dwellings, whole country vs urban); rent index is CPI actual rents (not market rents on new leases). Price-to-rent index base 2015 is an index, not a level yield.

## 2. OECD household balance sheets
- Financial (Table 720, non-consolidated, annual): https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.SDD.NAD,DSD_NASEC20@DF_T720R_A,1.1/A.N...S1M+S14.S1.N..LE...XDC......?format=csvfilewithlabels
  Raw raw/oecd_bs/t720r_a_hh_xdc.csv; clean clean/oecd_hh_financial_bs.csv (iso3,sector,entry[A=asset,L=liability,N=net],instrument,instrument_label,year,value,...); millions of national currency, current prices, end-year stocks.
  Instruments: F total, F2 currency & deposits, F3 debt securities, F4 loans, F5 equity & fund shares (F511 listed, F512 unlisted, F519 other, F52 fund shares, F521 MMF), F6 insurance/pension/guarantee (F62 life, F63 pension entitlements, F64, F65, F66), BF90 net financial worth.
  Sectors: S1M (households + NPISH): 43 areas, 1950-2025 (long history only USA 1950+, FRA); S14 (households alone): 38 areas, 1990-2025. Includes EU27_2020 aggregate.
- Non-financial (Table 9B / T2600, annual): https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.SDD.NAD,DSD_NASEC10@DF_TABLE9B,1.1/A..S14+S1M..........?format=csvfilewithlabels
  Raw raw/oecd_bs/table9b_s14_s1m.csv; clean clean/oecd_hh_nonfinancial_bs.csv; millions national currency, net (after depreciation), current prices.
  Items: N111N dwellings (35 countries), N211N land (30), N21111N land underlying dwellings (only 3), N2111N land under buildings (200 obs), NN total non-financial (only 10 countries), N1N produced, N2N non-produced, etc. S1M 37 countries 1959-2025, S14 11 countries 1970-2025.
- Script fetch_oecd_bs.py. Terms: OECD T&C (as above).
- Gaps/quirks: AUSTRALIA has non-financial (1988-2024) but NO financial balance sheet in T720 (financial not published in this flow). CHE and NOR have financial but no dwellings; JPN lacks F511/F512 split (F5 total only) ; most countries lack NN total -> sum N1N+N2N or use dwellings+land. Land underlying dwellings rarely split from land under all buildings. T720 is non-consolidated (gross asset and liability positions). Pension entitlements F63 are funded/DB/DC claims in the standard SNA (excludes unfunded social-security PAYG claims; see 4). Breaks at ESA 2010 / SNA 2008 revisions. NPISH included in S1M (small).
- Row-level caveat: always filter on entry (A/L/N). Rows also carry maturity (_Z = not applicable, T = total, S = short, L = long for F3/F4) and currency_denom (_T all currencies; a few XDC rows = national-currency-only breakdown): for totals use maturity in (_Z,T) and currency_denom == _T (then no duplicates on iso3,sector,entry,instrument,year; verified).

## 3. US Fed Z.1 B.101 (households & nonprofit organizations; table S1M.b)
- Primary: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/20260911/z1_csv_files.zip (Z.1 release 2026-09-11, data to 2026:Q2), file csv/S1M_b.csv + data_dictionary/S1M_b.txt. Raw raw/fred_z1/fed_zip/. Clean clean/us_z1_b101_households.csv (series_id, fred_id, label, period 'YYYY:Qn', value, unit). Script fetch_fred_z1.py.
- FRED keyless CSV (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=ID) is a mirror: id = BOGZ1 + code + Q (e.g. BOGZ1LM152010005Q), but many FRED ids differ or are not served. Verified-by-fetch and identical to the Fed file (max abs diff 0): TNWBSHNO (net worth = FL152090005), TABSHNO (total assets FL152000005), TFAABSHNO (financial assets FL154090005), TLBSHNO (liabilities FL154190005), HMLBSHNO (home mortgages FL153165105), HNOMFSA (mutual fund shares LM153064205), HNOREMV (real estate at market value LM155035005, last 54,073,947), BOGZ1LM152010005Q (nonfinancial assets), BOGZ1LM153064705Q (hedge fund shares), plus others in raw/fred_z1/*.csv. I could not find working FRED ids for deposits, corporate equities, pension entitlements, life insurance, owner-occupied real estate (BOGZ1FL...Q returned HTML); those come from the Fed file, which is the original source.
- Series kept (28): total assets, nonfinancial assets, real estate (market value; households' owner-occupied LM155035015), consumer durables, residential structures, financial assets, checkable deposits & currency FL153020005, time & savings deposits FL153030005, debt securities LM154022005, corporate equities LM153064105, other equity (IMA, i.e. private business) LM153081115, MMF shares, mutual fund shares LM153064205, hedge fund shares, life insurance reserves FL153040005, pension entitlements FL153050005, total liabilities, home mortgages FL153165105, consumer credit, net worth, owners' equity in real estate FL155035065 (+ % FL155035066).
- Coverage: annual 1945-1951 (year-end), quarterly 1952Q1-2026Q2; millions of USD, end of period, not seasonally adjusted. Terms: Fed/FRED public data, attribution to Board of Governors.
- Quirks: sector is households AND nonprofits (S1M); owners' equity is household-only real estate minus home mortgages (1-4 family); pension entitlements include state/local DB and are at the funding measure used by Z.1; last quarter(s) subject to revision.

## 4. Eurostat pension entitlements (national accounts Table 29)
- Datasets: nasa_10_pens1 (accrued-to-date pension entitlements in social insurance; core) and nasa_10_pens2 (by general-government sector), via https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nasa_10_pens1?format=SDMX-CSV&compressed=false (same for nasa_10_pens2). Raw raw/eurostat/. GDP: nama_10_gdp/A.CP_MEUR.B1GQ. (startPeriod=2010) -> raw/eurostat/nama_10_gdp.csv; clean/eurostat_gdp_current_meur.csv (31 countries + others mapped; 2010+).
- Clean: clean/eurostat_pension_entitlements_F63.csv (F63_LE closing and F63_LS opening entitlements; units MIO_EUR, MIO_NAC, PC_GDP) and eurostat_pension_entitlements_all.csv (all flows: contributions D61, benefits D62, D8 adjustment, revaluation K7, etc.). Script fetch_eurostat.py. build_balance.py computes PAYG % of GDP as Eurostat F63_LE (MIO_EUR) / nama_10_gdp B1GQ (MIO_EUR), falling back to World Bank LCU GDP where Eurostat GDP is missing. Terms: Eurostat free reuse with attribution (CC BY 4.0).
- Schemes: S13PS social security pension schemes (PAYG, unfunded); S13PBX gov-employee DB schemes outside core accounts; S13PBI gov-employee DB inside core; S13PC gov DC; S12PBI/S12PB/S12PC private DB/DC (funded); S1P total; S1PF funded; S13PU unfunded; S14R/S14NR resident/non-resident household counterparts.
- Coverage: 31 countries (EU27 + CH, IS, NO, UK; UK to 2018 only), reference years: all countries 2015, 2018, 2021 (the Table 29 triennial); annual in BG, CH, CZ, FI, HU, LT, LV, NL (to 2021), NO, SE; extra 2022-23 for CZ, DK, FI, LT, LV; some countries (RO 2021, UK 2021) missing. Example S1P F63_LE 2021: DE 12,769 bn EUR, FR 9,964 bn, IT 8,076 bn. PC_GDP unit gives share of GDP directly.
- Quirks: figures are present-value accrued-to-date entitlements under country-specific assumptions (discount rate, indexation) -> large cross-country differences; France 2021 below 2018 (discount-rate effect); nasa_10_pens2 is a sensitivity table (non-core government schemes), not additional entitlements; do not add S13PS to the S1P total without checking (S1P includes S13PS in this table).

## 5. Hours worked, nominal GDP
- PWT 11.0 (published 2025-10-07): https://dataverse.nl/api/access/datafile/554105 (Excel; page https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/; doi 10.34894/FABVLR). Raw raw/pwt/pwt110.xlsx. Clean clean/pwt110_hours.csv (variables emp, avh, hours_total_m = emp*avh in millions of hours, pop, hc, cgdpo, xr). Script fetch_pwt_wb_wpp.py. Licence CC BY 4.0.
- Coverage: 185 countries in file, 130 with emp and avh, 1950-2023. JPN emp 1950-2023. the author's composite_numeraire project/data already holds FRED PWT series (EMPENG{ISO2}A148NRUG, AVHWPE...; verified identical to PWT 11.0 (check/audit2/a3_pwt_vintage.py), so Sections 4/7 (FRED copies) and Section 6 (pwt110.xlsx) use the same hours. For 2024-25 the housing national hour extends total hours with World Bank labour-force growth (composite_numeraire/data/wb_lf.json; build_housing_ext.py).
- Quirks: avh is average annual hours per person engaged; PWT 11 revised employment/hours for many countries; many countries have avh only from 1950s-1990s; 2023 is the final year.
- World Bank: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CN (and .CD)?format=json&per_page=20000&date=1960:2025 ; raw raw/wb/; clean clean/worldbank_gdp.csv (iso3, year, variable, value, unit; aggregates dropped via the country list). Licence CC BY 4.0. Coverage: 214 countries 1960-2025; 2025 present for 186; JPN 2025 present. Quirks: latest year often preliminary; LCU series have base-currency redenominations (e.g. TUR, VEN, ZWE); USD uses market exchange rates.
